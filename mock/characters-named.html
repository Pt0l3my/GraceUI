Idrel

Ferry-warden

PlaythroughMemoriesConversations

General profile

What a first meeting shows. The same in every playthrough.

Keeps the rope ferry across the slow river below the downs, and has kept it longer than most of the bridges have stood. Wary of strangers who come after dark; generous with anyone who lends a hand with the rope.

Their way with news

Passes on what they hear to people they trust, and asks after the road in return.
