Spoken voices for NPCs. In this version NPCs speak in text.

In this version every NPC line appears as text: a floating label over the speaker, or the feed at the side of the screen.

Spoken voices arrive in v1.5. Nothing on this page can be set yet, so nothing here pretends to work.

Text and subtitles

What v1.5 adds

  • A voice for each named NPC and each type of NPC, from a speech engine that runs outside the game.
  • Lines paced by their audio, so the words on screen and the voice end together.
  • On this page then: the engine, its address, a test voice and the volume.

What never comes

  • Copies of the voices of the game’s own cast.
  • Sound from this menu: a voice preview will play through EldenNet’s own audio, only when you ask.
