Pacing

How often anyone speaks unasked, and how far lines reach.

Preset

What this means

No two unasked lines come closer than 2.5 s. In any minute, at most 6 lines in a fight, 2 idle remarks and 4 other unasked lines. NPCs start lines to you within 8 m; you overhear lines within 20 m and battle lines within 40 m.

Replies to you are never limited.

Gaps

Time between lines
2.5 s

The least time between any two unasked lines, whoever speaks them. Default: 2.5 s.

Time between lines from one fighting group
8 s

The least time between two battle lines from one group. Default: 8 s.

Window

Window length
60 s

The rolling stretch of play the counts below apply to. Default: 60 s.

Lines per window in a fight
6

Battle lines of every combatant, either side. Default: 6.

Idle remarks per window

Remarks about the moment from NPCs near you. Sparse is 2, normal is 4. Default: sparse.

Talk per window
4

Greetings, quest business, exchanges between NPCs and autonomy. Default: 4.

Reach

Speaking-to-you range
8 m

How near an NPC must be to start a line to you. Default: 8 m.

Overheard-lines range
20 m

How near lines between others must be for you to see them. Default: 20 m.

Combat-lines range
40 m

How near a fight must be for its lines to show. Default: 40 m.

Pacing

Pacing is the only limit on how often NPCs speak unasked: a gap between lines, a count within a window, and a cooldown for each kind of event.

How to use it

  1. Pick Quiet, Default or Lively to set every row at once.
  2. Or move a slider: “What this means” says the result in words.
  3. Replies to you are never limited by any of this.
