NPC types

Every NPC EldenNet may voice belongs to a kind and a type. Each type inherits what it may do from its kind, and you can narrow it here.

How to use it

  1. Pick a row to see its allowances at the right.
  2. Each allowance is Inherit, On or Off; the inherited value and where it comes from are shown under it.
  3. A gold lozenge marks a change; its reset button returns it to inherited.
  4. Every type is listed from the start, described as you would see one on a first meeting.
