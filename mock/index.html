Home

The dashboard: whether EldenNet is working, who is near you, what was just said, and what was asked of your model.

How to use it

  1. Read Status first: one word for the whole of EldenNet, then one dot for each model job.
  2. Setup holds your model, voice and microphone. It closes itself once the self-test has passed.
  3. Select a card's arrow to open its page.
  4. Customise shows, hides and orders the cards. The order is kept between sessions.
