Memories

Each named NPC you meet keeps a short journal of you: up to eight keepsakes, things they saw or things you told them, and facts EldenNet counts.

How to use it

  1. Choose an NPC to read their journal, what they think of you and the facts EldenNet keeps.
  2. Weight is how much a keepsake matters to them; the protected one is never dropped when the journal is rewritten.
  3. Forget removes one NPC’s memories, or the whole journey’s. Each takes a backup and asks you twice.
  4. Nothing here can be edited: memories come only from what happened.
