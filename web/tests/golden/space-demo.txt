  Handbook
  The keeper's handbook: how to light, feed and keep the lamps.
  GUIDES
  Lighting the lamp
  Before dusk · The match · Trimming
  Oil
  Which oil · Storing it
  Keeping a lamp in a storm
  Wind · Rain · When to shutter
  GUIDE
  Lighting the lamp
  A lamp lit well burns all night with one trim. Read Oil first if the lamp is new to you.
  Before dusk
  Clean the glass inside and out.
  Fill the oil to the line, never above it.
  Check the wick is dry.
  The match
  Strike the match away from the glass, and light the wick at its edge, not its middle.
  Never leave a lit lamp alone in its first quarter hour.
  Trimming
  Trim to a flat, even wick; a peaked wick smokes. In wind, see Keeping a lamp in a storm.
